# 2026 CFB Model

Weekly ratings and bets from SP+ and BCF Toys FEI projections blended with the betting market. Each week folder holds that week's actionable bets (README.md, bets.csv) and the model's ratings for every game (ratings.md, ratings.csv).

## Record

Posted bets are frozen when the week is written and graded at the price shown. Backfilled weeks were generated after the games from the walk-forward paper record (fit on earlier seasons only) and were not posted before kickoff. Closing-number picks are graded at the median line across books, which can be a quarter point no book offered. Projection publish times are estimated (an hour before each week's first kickoff), not recorded.

## Rules

- **Totals, early number** (headline): bet the over or under at the O/U printed in SP+'s weekly sheet when the expected value at −110 is at least +2%.
- **Spreads, your book now**: bet a side at FanDuel, DraftKings or Fanatics when it clears +2% EV. The spread market already prices SP+ and FEI, so these are rare.
- Backfilled weeks also show the record's closing-number rules.
- Model, backtests and caveats: see the repository README and `reports/`.
