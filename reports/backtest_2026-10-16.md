### Reality check

- Break-even at -110 is 52.38%.
- Standard error of a win rate over N bets is about 0.5/sqrt(N): with N = 453, that is 2.3% (about 2.2 points at N = 500). A 54% backtest on a few hundred bets is not distinguishable from break-even.
- CLV is the faster, more reliable signal.
- These results are vs the **retail consensus close** (median of CFBD providers), not a sharp close.
